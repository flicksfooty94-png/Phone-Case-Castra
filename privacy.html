This policy explains what personal data Holdfast collects when you browse the shop or place an order, why we collect it, and what rights you have.

Who we are

The shop is run by [Legal business name], [Registered business address]. You can reach us at [Contact email].

What we collect

  • Order details. Your name, email address, shipping address, billing address and, if you give it, phone number. We need these to process and deliver your order.
  • Payment details. Payments are handled by [Payment processor]. Your card details go to them directly. We do not see or store full card numbers.

What is stored in your browser

To remember your cart and your chosen phone model between pages, the shop saves that information in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent to us until you check out. The shop does not set advertising cookies or analytics cookies, and it does not load fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers.

How we use your data

  • To take payment, fulfil and deliver your order, and handle returns or refunds.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the shop secure.
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax and accounting records.

If you are in a place where a legal basis is required, we rely on performing our contract with you, meeting legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in running a secure shop.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with the services that help us complete your order: [Payment processor] for payment, [Shipping carriers] for delivery, and [Hosting provider] for hosting. They may only use your data to provide those services. We may also disclose data when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal data.

International transfers

Some of these services may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we use safeguards recognised by law for those transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for [Retention period], because tax and accounting rules require it. Emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up. After that we delete or anonymise the data.
